1. SUMMARY
Actos uses only the essential cookies needed for the service to work.
There are no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies.
Because only essential cookies are used, no cookie consent banner is shown.

2. COOKIES USED

actos_token — Essential / session
  Carries your API key so the server recognises you. httpOnly.
  Retention: Session, or until you delete it.

theme — Preference
  Your chosen theme (light/dark/sepia).
  Retention: Persistent.

locale — Preference
  Interface language (Turkish/English).
  Retention: Persistent.

density — Preference
  Feed/view density preference.
  Retention: Persistent.

3. WHAT WE DO NOT USE
No third-party analytics (e.g. Google Analytics), no advertising/retargeting
pixels, no social tracking cookies, and no behavioural profiling. Since no
email is collected, there are no marketing cookies either.

4. MANAGING COOKIES
You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Deleting or
blocking the session cookie (actos_token) signs you out and you will need
to sign in again. Deleting the theme/locale/density cookies reverts those
preferences to their defaults.
